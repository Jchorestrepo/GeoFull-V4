# Brag Plan: GeoFull V4

## What is this app?
GeoFull V4 is an ultra-fast, multi-tenant spatial intelligence platform featuring 7-level precision PostGIS geocoding, multi-zone sectorization, and automated delivery reconciliation.

## The angle
Sleek Apple Dark Minimalist product launch film highlighting high-precision spatial processing across 518,000+ catastral properties in milliseconds.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Deep dark background with glowing titanium blue typography: "518,000+ PROPERTIES. 7 LEVELS OF PRECISION." exploding into a glassmorphic spatial radar view.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Pipeline Processing**: 3-Stage Pipeline: `Sanitizer (V3)` → `PostGIS 7-Level Geocoder` → `ST_Contains Sectorizer`.
- **Live Sectorization Metrics**: Glassmorphic Apple cards showing 100% Exact Precision (Emerald Green), Close Range (Amber), Out of Zone (Crimson).
- **Automated Delivery Reconciliation**: Instant delivery verification & payroll settlement dashboard with live status counters.

## Outro / punchline
"GeoFull V4. Spatial intelligence, refined." Logo & CTA landing over sleek dark canvas.

## User flow worth showing
Upload CSV/Excel -> Automatic PostGIS 7-Level Geocoding & Sectorization -> Instant Live Map & Delivery Settlement metrics.
